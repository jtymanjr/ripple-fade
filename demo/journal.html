Long text, a gradient background and a different palette.

The pond was flat enough to hold the whole evening. Then something small broke the surface, and the picture went out from it in rings, each one carrying a little more of the next scene, until the last ring settled and the new page was simply there.

That is the whole idea. The old page is photographed the moment you click, the new page loads underneath, and the photograph is rippled away over it. Nothing on the new page has to know about it.
