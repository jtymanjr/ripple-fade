RippleFade

Ripple experiments

One transition, built four ways. A ripple dropped on a surface carries picture A into picture B, ring by ring. Each experiment below makes that ripple with a different technique.

Scenarios

three.jsWebGLwave simulation

Technique

The ripple shader runs on the surfaces of 3D objects. Flat surfaces are driven by a real wave-equation simulation on the GPU, so rings bounce off the edges and interfere.

Inside

  • Ten scenes, including a pond, a puddle, a warp gate, a phone, a gallery wall, a pool, a cauldron, a planet, a sci-fi console and twin forests
  • Render styles: ASCII, low-poly dither, topo map, ridgelines and slices
  • Effect toggles, camera views, auto-drip and your own A and B images

Simulator

WebGLfragment shader

Technique

The original 2D version: one full-screen shader blends image A into image B behind an expanding train of rings, bending A as each ring passes and adding a sheen on the crests.

Inside

  • Sliders for ripples, wavelength, speed, strength, refraction, sheen and edge softness
  • Load or drop your own A and B images

Page transitions

ripple-fade.jshtml2canvasWebGL

Technique

A drop-in script for real websites. On a link click the page photographs itself with html2canvas. The next page puts that photo up before it paints, then the shader ripples it away to reveal the live page.

Inside

  • A four-page demo site: home, gallery, journal and an in-page (single-page app) transition
  • Opt-in or opt-out links, and a code API for single-page apps

CSS ripple, no WebGL

View TransitionsCSS masksSVG filter

Technique

No canvas at all. The browser snapshots the old page, animated radial-gradient masks fade it in rings, highlight and shadow layers light the rings from one side, and an SVG displacement filter adds the refraction from small maps baked on click.

Inside

  • A two-page demo: click anywhere to ripple between them
  • Add ?light=0 or ?refract=0 to the address to compare without lighting or refraction
  • Tested in Chrome; browsers without View Transitions swap pages instantly
